# 03 — Evidence Mapping

## Clinical Trial

The central clinical evidence for the assessment comes from the CLARITY AD trial.

However, the overall trial population does not automatically correspond to the populations and comparator therapies required for the German early benefit assessment.

The analysis therefore requires mapping the clinical evidence to the specific HTA research questions.

---

## Research Question 1

### MCI due to Alzheimer's disease

**Intervention**

Lecanemab

**Comparator**

Watchful waiting

The relevant evidence was assessed in the population corresponding to the defined comparator situation.

---

## Research Question 2

### Mild Alzheimer's dementia

**Intervention**

Lecanemab + acetylcholinesterase inhibitor

**Comparator**

Placebo + acetylcholinesterase inhibitor

The relevant subgroup consisted of patients who were receiving acetylcholinesterase inhibitor therapy at baseline.

This comparison is important because it aligns the clinical evidence with the German ZVT.

---

## Research Question 3

### Mild Alzheimer's dementia — monotherapy

**Intervention**

Lecanemab monotherapy

**Comparator**

Acetylcholinesterase inhibitor therapy

No relevant comparative study was identified for this treatment situation.

Therefore, the available evidence could not establish an added-benefit conclusion for this comparison.

---

## Key Analytical Observation

The distinction between the overall clinical trial population and the relevant ZVT-conforming populations is one of the most important lessons from this case.

A treatment effect observed in the overall trial cannot simply be transferred to a different treatment setting.

The evidence must first answer the specific PICO question defined for the HTA assessment.
