# 02 — PICO and Zweckmäßige Vergleichstherapie

## PICO Framework

PICO provides a structured way to define the clinical question.

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| P | Population |
| I | Intervention |
| C | Comparator |
| O | Outcomes |

---

## Population

The IQWiG assessment considered patients with early Alzheimer's disease, including:

- Mild cognitive impairment (MCI) due to Alzheimer's disease
- Mild Alzheimer's dementia

The relevant populations were restricted according to the characteristics specified for the indication, including ApoE ε4 status.

---

## Intervention

The intervention was:

**Lecanemab**

Lecanemab is an anti-amyloid monoclonal antibody used for the treatment of early Alzheimer's disease in the relevant authorised population.

---

## Comparator

The comparator is particularly important because German HTA does not simply compare a new medicine against placebo regardless of treatment context.

The appropriate comparator is the **Zweckmäßige Vergleichstherapie (ZVT)**.

### MCI due to Alzheimer's disease

ZVT:

**Watchful waiting (beobachtendes Abwarten)**

### Mild Alzheimer's dementia

ZVT:

**Acetylcholinesterase inhibitor therapy**

Examples include:

- Donepezil
- Galantamine
- Rivastigmine

---

## Outcome Domains

The assessment considers patient-relevant outcomes including:

### Mortality

Whether treatment affects survival.

### Morbidity

Including disease progression and symptoms.

### Health-Related Quality of Life

Including measures such as EQ-5D and QOL-AD.

### Adverse Events

Including treatment-related harms and serious adverse events.

---

## Why PICO Matters

The PICO framework prevents a common analytical error:

> treating evidence from a broader clinical trial population as automatically applicable to the reimbursement question.

The relevant question is not simply:

**"Does lecanemab work?"**

It is:

**"Does lecanemab provide an added benefit compared with the appropriate German comparator in the relevant patient population and for patient-relevant outcomes?"**

This distinction is central to HTA evidence appraisal.
